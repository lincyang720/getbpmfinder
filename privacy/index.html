Privacy Policy

Audio stays on your device

When you select an audio file in BPM Finder, your browser reads and analyzes the file on your device. The browser handles the audio decoding and derives the tempo and key from the decoded sound. The resulting BPM, key, Camelot number, duration, and confidence are calculated locally and displayed in your browser. BPM Finder does not upload, collect, or save your audio files. This applies to the supported formats, including MP3, WAV, OGG, and M4A.

If you use tap tempo, you can estimate the beat from your taps without selecting an audio file. You can choose this mode whenever you want to check a tempo by ear.

Accounts and profiling

You can open BPM Finder, analyze a track, and use tap tempo without registering or signing in. The tool does not ask you to enter a name or email address to access a detection result. BPM Finder does not create user profiles based on your use of the tool.

Routine server access logs

Your browser makes ordinary web requests to load the site and its resources. The site's server logs are limited to routine access logs for those web requests. Loading the page and analyzing a file are separate operations. The server handles the request for the page, and your device handles the audio analysis. Your selected audio file stays on your device and is not part of those server requests.

If you have a question about local processing or server access logs, you can reach BPM Finder at hi@getbpmfinder.com. You can describe your question without attaching an audio file.
