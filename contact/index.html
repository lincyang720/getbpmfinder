Email BPM Finder

For questions about how to use BPM Finder, suggestions for making it easier to use, or problems you encounter while analyzing a track or tapping a beat, email hi@getbpmfinder.com. In the subject line, name the feature and briefly describe the issue or suggestion. If you have a question about an estimate, say which part of the result you are trying to understand.

What to include in a bug report

Include the audio file format, such as MP3 or M4A, and the browser you were using. If you know the browser version, include that too. Describe the steps you took and what you expected to happen. For a detection issue, share the result you saw, such as the BPM, key, or confidence value. If no result appeared, describe any error message or where the analysis stopped.

For a tap tempo issue, tell us what happened as you tapped and what BPM appeared, if any. A short description of what you saw is enough to start. Please leave the audio file out of your email.
