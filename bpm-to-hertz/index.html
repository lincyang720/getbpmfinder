How many Hz is 1 BPM

1 BPM = 0.01667 Hz (rounded). In the other direction, 1 Hz = 60 BPM, and 60 BPM = 1 Hz. Divide BPM by 60 to get Hz. Multiply Hz by 60 to get BPM.

Note values as LFO rates

Count a quarter note as one beat and allow one complete LFO cycle per note. Divide BPM by 60, then divide by the note length in beats. To sync an LFO to eighth notes at 120 BPM, set its rate to 4 Hz.

A dot adds half the original duration, so a dotted quarter takes 1.5 beats. A quarter-note triplet fits three notes into two beats, giving each cycle just two-thirds of a beat. The dotted sweep takes longer and the triplet sweep repeats faster.

BPM vs Hz: what's the difference

A DAW uses beats per minute to place musical events on a grid. A synthesizer uses hertz to describe how many times an oscillator completes its waveform each second. Beat frequency is the reciprocal of the time between beats in seconds. At 120 BPM the gap is 0.5 seconds, so a pulse on every beat repeats twice a second.

Tempo and oscillator frequency describe different events. To connect them, decide what one cycle should span. A filter can open and close four times during a single beat while the drummer keeps exactly the same tempo.

Frequently asked questions

How many Hz is 1 BPM?

One beat per minute is one cycle every 60 seconds. Its frequency is 1/60 Hz, approximately 0.01667 Hz. Keep the fraction when you need an exact value.

Can I use a Hz reading directly as BPM?

Multiply the reading by 60 if each cycle represents one beat. For example, a light flashing twice a second marks 120 beats per minute if you count every flash.

Why do synthesizer LFOs use Hz?

An LFO can run at a fixed speed even when no song clock is available. A rate in Hz tells you how often its waveform repeats. Tempo sync instead lets you select a musical division that follows project tempo changes.

Do heart or breathing rates correspond to song BPM?

You can express any of these counts per minute or per second. Matching the numbers only compares timing. A song tempo alone tells you nothing about an individual listener’s heart or breathing rate.

Does audio sample rate affect the conversion?

No. Sample rate counts digital measurements of audio per second. Changing a recording session from 44.1 kHz to 48 kHz leaves the calculation for a given song tempo unchanged.

How do I keep an eighth-note filter sweep in time?

At 120 BPM, a beat lasts half a second. An eighth note lasts a quarter of a second, so set the LFO to 4 Hz. To align the start of each sweep with the grid, also check the synth’s phase or retrigger setting.
